    Two arms.
    One rule.

    A via ferrata is a climbing route of steel cables, iron rungs and bridges bolted into the rock. Your lanyard has two arms, and at least one is always clipped to the cable. That's the whole system.

    • Clip bothBoth carabiners on the cable before you step off the ground.
    • Move oneAt each bolt, unclip one arm, clip it past, then move the other.
    • Never zeroYou never have both arms off the cable at the same time.

    Book it before
    you lose signal.

    No counter and no shop hours. Everything happens on your phone before you reach the lot.

    1. Before you drive

      Pick your dates

      One day (24 hours) or two (48 hours). You'll see how many kits are left on each day.

    2. On your couch

      Watch and sign

      Watch the safety video to the end, then every adult signs the agreement online.

    3. Before you lose signal

      Save your pass

      Your locker door and code go on a pass you keep on your phone. There's no service at the gorge.

    4. In the parking lot

      Open, gear up, go

      Punch in your code, grab your kit and start the approach.

    5. On your way out

      Lock it back up

      Return everything to the same locker before your time runs out.

    Gear check.

    No cell service at the gorge

    Save your pass and directions before you leave Vernal.

    County waiver too

    Uintah County requires its own waiver for every climber. Sign it here.

    2 liters of water each

    Most of the route is in full sun and there's no water at the trailhead.

    Real shoes

    Hiking or approach shoes with good grip. No sandals.

    Start early

    Afternoon storms build fast, and lightning and steel cables don't mix.

    Gloves and snacks

    Cables get hot and rough. There's nowhere to buy food nearby.
