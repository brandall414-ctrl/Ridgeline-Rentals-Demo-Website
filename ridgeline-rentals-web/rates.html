Deposit or Gear Protection

Every rental needs one of these. You choose at checkout.

Card hold. A refundable hold on your card ($150 per kit, $80 per lanyard), released the same day your gear is back in the locker.

Gear Protection. Pay a small fee instead ($8 per kit, $5 per lanyard) and skip the hold. It covers accidental damage from normal use, not misuse or gear that isn't returned.

Who can climb

  • Weight between 90 and 265 lbs
  • Vertical reach of at least 62 inches
  • 18 or older to rent. Ages 14 to 17 can climb with a parent or guardian on the route
  • Signed Trailside rental agreement and Uintah County waiver
  • Safety video watched all the way through before signing

Returns and late fees

A 1-day rental is due back in the locker within 24 hours of pickup, a 2-day rental within 48 hours. Late returns are charged another day's rate for each 24 hours past that. Unreturned gear is charged at replacement cost: $50 helmet, $60 harness, $80 lanyard.

Read before you sign

Every renter signs our rental agreement and liability release during booking. You can read it now.
